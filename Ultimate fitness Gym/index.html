Fitness kya hai?

Fitness ka matlab sirf bade muscles ya patla shareer nahi. Iska matlab hai ki aapka shareer roz ke kaam aaram se kar sake, aur dimaag tanav mein bhi shaant rahe.

Regular exercise ke fayde


  • Dil aur phephde majboot hote hain
  • Weight control mein rehta hai
  • Haddiyan aur muscles strong hote hain
  • Neend achhi aati hai, stress kam hota hai
  • Energy aur confidence badhta hai
